What is IELTS?

The International English Language Testing System (IELTS) is the world's most popular high-stakes English language proficiency test, recognised by over 11,000 organisations globally — including universities, employers, immigration authorities, and professional bodies.

🎓

Academic

For those applying to undergraduate or postgraduate programmes, or seeking professional registration. Requires a higher level of academic English.

  • University admission
  • Professional registration
  • Migration to Australia, Canada, NZ
✈️

General Training

For those migrating to English-speaking countries, or applying for secondary education and work experience programmes.

  • Work and migration (UK, Canada, Australia)
  • Secondary school programmes
  • Training programmes

Band Score Scale

IELTS is scored on a 9-band scale, from 1 (Non User) to 9 (Expert User). Each band reflects the test taker's level of English proficiency.

8Very Good
7Good
