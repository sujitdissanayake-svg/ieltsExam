Instructions to candidates

  • Mock Test → Means you have access to complete one full test (Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking with an examiner).
  • Access → Refers to the duration (e.g., 1 week or 2 week) during which you can retake the purchased exam.
  • Attempts → Refers to how many times you can retake the purchased test within the access period.
    • Attempts include Listening, Reading, and Writing.
    • Writing is evaluated by an examiner each time.
    • Attempts do not include the live Speaking session with an examiner.

🎯 Mock Test Attempts

  • These are full mock test attempts where all four components are evaluated.
  • You will receive examiner reports for Writing and Speaking, along with band scores.

✍️ Can Practise For

  • You may rewrite answers for Listening, Reading, and Writing.
  • Band scores are provided only for Listening and Reading.
  • The Writing platform can be used for practice, but answers will not be evaluated or assigned a band score.
  • Repeated Speaking tests with an examiner are not available under this option.
